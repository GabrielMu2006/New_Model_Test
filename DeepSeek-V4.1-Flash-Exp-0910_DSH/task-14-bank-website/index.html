Common questions

Before you open an account

How long does it take to open an account?

About five minutes online for a personal checking or savings account. You'll need a government ID, your Social Security number, and a US address. Most accounts are approved instantly; if we need a second look, you'll hear from us within one business day.

Is there really no monthly fee?

Everyday Checking is $0 per month when you receive at least $500 in qualifying direct deposits each month. Without direct deposit it's $8 per month, waived if you keep a $1,500 average daily balance. High-Yield Savings has no monthly fee and no minimum balance, ever.

What happens if I overdraw my account?

We decline the transaction and charge nothing. You can opt into a fee-free overdraft buffer of up to $50 on debit card purchases, which you repay automatically with your next deposit. We never reorder transactions to generate fees.

Are my deposits insured?

Deposits are insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category. Joint accounts are insured up to $500,000. If you hold more than that, our team can help you structure coverage across ownership categories.

Can I keep my old bank account for a while?

Yes. Open the Meridian account, move your direct deposit when you're ready, and use our switch kit to redirect recurring payments. We'll show you a checklist and keep both accounts open as long as you like — there's no penalty for leaving, and no penalty for staying.
