Calibre AE-39

Three hundred and forty-two parts,
and not one of them hurried.

Designed in-house, decorated by hand, and regulated in six positions over eleven days before it is allowed to leave the atelier.

  • 01

    Free-sprung balance

    No regulator index. Rate is set by four solid gold timing screws, which keeps the amplitude stable as the reserve falls.

  • 02

    Seventy hours

    A single barrel, wound by a tungsten micro-rotor, holds three days of running - long enough to set the watch down on Friday.

  • 03

    Anglage by hand

    Every bridge is bevelled on a rotating wooden lap, then black-polished at the steel. Eleven hours per movement.

  • 04

    Finished twice

    Assembled to prove the geometry, disassembled, cleaned, and assembled again for the client. Nothing is shipped on a first build.

The atelier

Nine watchmakers.
One room above the lake.

Aevum began in 2011 when three watchmakers left the Vallee de Joux for a converted boathouse outside Geneva. We make one thing, slowly. The rose engine that cuts our dials was built in 1911 and has never left the room.

A watch should not shout the hour it was bought. It should simply keep it. Camille Aevum, founder
