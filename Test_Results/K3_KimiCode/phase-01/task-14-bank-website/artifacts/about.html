Our story

A bank built by its depositors

Meridian was founded by forty-one schoolteachers and dockworkers who pooled $8,400 to lend to neighbors the big banks would not see. Every depositor still owns an equal vote in how the bank is run — one member, one vote, regardless of balance.

We grew slowly on purpose. No acquisitions sprees, no subprime desk, no bailouts. The 2008 crisis cost us a flat year; it cost some of our competitors their customers' trust. We think slow was the right speed.

Today the membership looks different — developers and nurses, bakeries and machine shops — but the rule above the desk has not changed.

  • 1948

    Forty-one founders open the first Meridian storefront with $8,400 in pooled deposits.

  • 1965

    First mortgage issued to a member family — a $9,200 loan at 5.5%, paid off in 1987.

  • 1984

    Membership passes 10,000. The board votes down a sale to a regional bank, 9 to 2.

  • 2009

    Meridian posts a profitable crisis year and opens two new branches while peers retrench.

  • 2019

    Monthly maintenance fees abolished across every personal account. For good.

  • 2026

    High-Yield Savings reaches 4.60% APY — the highest member rate in our history.

Values

What we optimize for

Local first

Deposits fund loans in the communities they came from. Your savings build your neighbor's bakery, not a distant trading desk.

Plain language

Every fee and every rate fits on one page. If a disclosure needs a lawyer to read, we rewrite the product, not the font size.

One member, one vote

Board seats are elected by members, not purchased by shareholders. A $1 saver and a $1M saver vote as equals.
