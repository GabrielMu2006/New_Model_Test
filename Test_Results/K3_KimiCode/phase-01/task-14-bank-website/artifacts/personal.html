Questions

Frequently asked

Is Meridian Bank real?

No. Meridian Bank is a fictional demonstration website. The products, rates, and stories here are illustrative. Never enter real personal information or credentials.

How does the 4.60% APY work?

In this demo, the rate is simply a headline number. In a real high-yield account, interest would compound daily and post monthly, with the APY reflecting that compounding over a year.

What does “no overdraft fees under $25” mean?

If a purchase takes your balance no more than $25 negative, we let it through without a fee. Beyond that, purchases are declined unless you link savings for automatic, free overdraft transfers.

Can I open a joint account?

In a real version, yes — both owners get full visibility and either can manage the account. In this demo, everything is a front-end illustration with fictional data.

How do virtual card numbers work?

The app can generate a unique card number per online store, so a breach at one merchant never exposes your real card. Charges still appear on one statement.
