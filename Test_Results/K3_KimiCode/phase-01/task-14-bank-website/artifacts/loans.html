The process

What borrowing here looks like

1 · Check your rate

A two-minute pre-qualification with a soft credit pull that never affects your score.

2 · Lock it in

Like the number? Lock it. Mortgage locks are free for 60 days; personal and auto for 45.

3 · Sign online

Every document is e-signable. Mortgages close in person only if you want them to.

No prepayment penalty on anything we lend. Extra payments always go to principal.
