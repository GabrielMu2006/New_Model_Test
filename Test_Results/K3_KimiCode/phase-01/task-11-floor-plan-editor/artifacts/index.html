Help

  • W — draw walls: click to start, click to finish, chain to continue, Esc/Enter stop, Shift forces straight
  • D / N — place a door / window on a wall
  • V — select; drag walls, endpoints, doors and windows
  • Double-click a door to flip its swing
  • Space+drag or middle mouse — pan; wheel — zoom
  • Del — delete selection; Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y — undo/redo
