03 — Heritage

Ninety-nine years.
Three hundred pieces a year.

  1. 1927

    The bench above the Rhône

    Aurélien Voss, twenty-six, opens a two-man atelier in Genève with one rule: never make more than can be finished properly.

  2. 1953

    Calibre AV-1

    The maison's first in-house movement — 72 hours of reserve when forty was the convention.

  3. 1978

    The quiet refusal

    As the quartz crisis empties the Vallée, Aevum declines to electrify. Production falls to 211 pieces. The bench survives.

  4. 2004

    Céleste

    A moonphase geared to drift one day in 122 years — longer than anyone who buys one will own it.

  5. 2026

    Unchanged, on purpose

    Three calibres, three hundred pieces, one waiting list. The rule from 1927 still holds.

«We do not count the hours.
We give them a home.»

— Aurélien Voss, Founder · Genève, 1927
