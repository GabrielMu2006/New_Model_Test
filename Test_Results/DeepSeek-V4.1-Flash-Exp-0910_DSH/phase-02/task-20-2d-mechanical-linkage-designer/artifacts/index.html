How to use the linkage designer

Build a mechanism

  1. Fixed pivot — click on the canvas to pin a joint to the ground.
  2. Rotating joint — click to place a free pin joint.
  3. Rigid link — click one joint, then another. Type a length and tick use value to create a link of an exact length; leave it empty to use the drawn distance. Clicking empty space creates the joint for you.
  4. Track — click two points to lay down a rail, then use Slider and click near the rail to put a slider on it.
  5. Motor — click a joint that has a link attached; that link becomes the driven crank. Change the driven link and the speed in the inspector.
  6. Delete — click any joint, link, track or motor to remove it.

Simulate

  • Play / Pause (Space) runs the motor; Step (S) advances one frame; Reset (R) returns to the design pose.
  • 2° buttons and the crank angle slider move the driven link directly.
  • Drag any joint with the mouse: the solver keeps every link rigid and every slider on its rail while you pull.
  • Motion paths are drawn for every moving joint; Clear paths wipes them.

Good to know

  • Rigid links keep their exact length at every step — the status bar shows the worst length error in the current pose (it should read 0.000).
  • Sliders stay on their rails; joints stay attached to every link that meets there.
  • If a motor cannot reach the commanded angle (hard stop, toggle position, jamming linkage) the simulation stops and tells you which constraint blocked it.
  • Tracks are ground-fixed rails. A slider riding on a moving link is not supported in this version.
