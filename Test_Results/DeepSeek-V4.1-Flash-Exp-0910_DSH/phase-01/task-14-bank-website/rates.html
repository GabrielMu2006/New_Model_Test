How we set deposit rates

Our savings and CD rates track the federal funds rate and what we need to fund loans. We publish changes at 9:00 a.m. ET and never change a CD rate mid-term.

APY vs. APR

APY is what you earn on deposits, including compounding. APR is what you pay on credit, excluding compounding but including most fees. They're not comparable numbers.

Your rate is the published rate

No teaser periods, no "up to" language. If you qualify for the account, you get the rate in this table on the day you open it.
