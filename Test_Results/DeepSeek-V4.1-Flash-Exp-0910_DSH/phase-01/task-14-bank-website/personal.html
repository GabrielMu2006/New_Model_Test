The fine print, in plain English

What we charge for — and what we don't

Everyday Checking fees
  • $0 monthly maintenance with qualifying direct deposit (otherwise $8, waived with a $1,500 average daily balance).
  • $0 overdraft fee. We decline the transaction and charge nothing.
  • $0 for the first 20 ATM transactions per month at out-of-network machines; $2.50 after that.
  • $0 for incoming wires, cashier's checks, and stop-payment requests placed in the app.
  • $25 for an outgoing domestic wire, $45 international.
  • $12 to replace a lost debit card with expedited shipping; standard shipping is free.
Savings and term savings fees
  • $0 monthly fee and $0 minimum balance on High-Yield Savings.
  • $0 for transfers between your Meridian accounts, no matter how many.
  • $500 minimum to open a Term Savings account; $0 account fee.
  • Early withdrawal from a Term Savings account costs three months of interest.
  • Withdrawals above six per month from savings are allowed — we don't charge for them, but federal rules may require us to convert the account to checking.
Credit card fees
  • $0 annual fee on Cashback and Secure cards. Voyage is $95, waived the first year.
  • $0 foreign transaction fees on every card.
  • Late payment: up to $40, never more than your minimum payment.
  • Cash advance: $10 or 3%, whichever is greater, plus interest from the day of the advance.
  • Returned payment: $29.
