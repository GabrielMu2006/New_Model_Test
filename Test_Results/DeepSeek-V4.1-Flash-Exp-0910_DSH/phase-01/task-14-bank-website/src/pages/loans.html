Loan questions

Answers before you apply

Will checking my rate hurt my credit score?

No. Checking a rate with Meridian uses a soft inquiry, which is invisible to other lenders and doesn't affect your score. A full application uses a hard inquiry, typically a five-point dip that recovers within a few months.

How much house can I afford?

A common guideline is keeping total housing costs below 28% of gross monthly income and total debt below 36%. Our calculator lets you test any combination — the mortgage payment above includes taxes and insurance so you can compare it directly to your budget.

Can I pay extra without a penalty?

Yes, on every loan we offer. Extra payments go straight to principal by default, and you can set up a recurring extra amount in the app. On a $360,000 mortgage at 6.375%, an extra $200 a month saves roughly $118,000 in interest and pays the loan off about six years early.

What documents do I need for a mortgage?
  • Two recent pay stubs and your last two W-2s, or two years of tax returns if self-employed.
  • Two months of statements for every asset account you want counted.
  • Your government ID and Social Security number.
  • For a purchase: the signed purchase contract once you have one.
Do you offer loans to self-employed borrowers?

Yes. We underwrite self-employed borrowers using two years of filed returns, and we can also consider 12 or 24 months of bank statements through our stated-income program for established businesses. Ask your loan officer which path fits your situation.
