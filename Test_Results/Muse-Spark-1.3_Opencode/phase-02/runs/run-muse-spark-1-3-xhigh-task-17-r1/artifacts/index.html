Every mesh alternates direction. Speed ratios equal inverse tooth ratios, all derived from a single movementTime. Escape steps once per half balance period (beat 0.8 s); its 12 s mean period matches the continuous train average.

How to read it

Mainspring spiral in the barrel drives the train barrel → center → third → fourth → escape. The escape wheel is locked and released by the pallet fork, which is driven by the balance wheel. Balance oscillates ±270°, pallet rocks ±12°, escape advances one tooth (24°) per beat with a short impulse and lock. Hour hand = minute/12, second hand = minute×60 on the sub-dial.

Press Space to pause. Drag the speed slider 0.1–5× to time-lapse the whole movement at once.
