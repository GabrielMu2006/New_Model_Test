The Maison

A century of quiet obsession.

Founded in 1897 by watchmaker Auguste Veyre, Aevum — from aevum, the time of angels — has remained wilfully small. One valley. One atelier. Twenty-three artisans who sign every bridge they bevel.

“We do not make watches to tell the time. We make them to honour it.”

— Auguste Veyre, 1902 notebook
