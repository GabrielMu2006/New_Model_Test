▦ FloorPlan Studio

draw walls · doors & windows · drag furniture · live dimensions
How to use
• W drag on canvas = wall (hold Shift = straight). Length shows live.
• D/N drag = door / window. Overlaps cut a gap in the wall.
• V select: drag bodies to move, drag ● endpoints to stretch, Del removes.
• Furniture: click a library item, then drag items anywhere.
• M measure: drag between two points.
