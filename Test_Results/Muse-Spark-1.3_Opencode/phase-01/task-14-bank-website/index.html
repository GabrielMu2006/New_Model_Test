    Questions, answered

    Still stuck? Call 1-800-555-0199 — real humans, 24/7.

    Is my money FDIC insured?

    Yes — in this demo we state deposits would be FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category, as with any real US member bank.

    Are there really no overdraft fees?

    Everyday Checking has no overdraft fees. Transactions that would overdraw are simply declined (or covered by free overdraft transfer from savings if you enable it).

    How long does it take to open an account?

    About 5 minutes online with a photo ID. Most accounts are approved instantly; funding via debit card or ACH is free.

    Can I freeze my card if I lose it?

    Yes — one tap in the app or online banking. Unfreeze the same way. A replacement virtual card is issued instantly.
