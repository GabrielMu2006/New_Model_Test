Good questions

Is Morrow a real bank?+

Morrow is a fictional banking concept created for this demo. In a real deployment, charter, partner-bank, and FDIC disclosures would appear here.

Are there monthly account fees?+

The products shown in this concept have no monthly maintenance fees or minimum balance requirements.

How quickly can I open an account?+

A typical digital application takes a few minutes. You would need a government-issued ID and basic personal information.

How is my money protected?+

Real-time alerts, multi-factor authentication, encryption, and FDIC deposit insurance would work together to protect eligible accounts.
